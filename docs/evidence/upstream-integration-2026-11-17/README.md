# Integration observations — 2026-11-17

These files accompany `docs/UPSTREAM_INTEGRATION_2026-11-17.md`. They record a **partial, unaccepted candidate**. They are not production translation approvals, qualification records, or replacements for historical research evidence.

- `revision-receipt.json` identifies executable revisions, merge parents, unchanged protected branches, identical frontend trees, packaged metadata, and the local final JUnit archive hash. Documentation commits follow the tested executable application revision.
- `native-test-summary.json` and `application-test-summary.json` aggregate final JUnit XMLs. Their timestamps and sorted XML-manifest hashes identify the observations. `application-failure-details.json` retains every failed case name, grouped with a representative original message/stack trace. Failures remain failures.
- `catalog-verification.json` distinguishes the original raw Windows failure, its earlier diagnostic replay, and the final-head replay. `catalog-line-ending-analysis.json` retains the original per-snapshot Git-blob/working-copy/actual hashes. Canonicalization touches copied build resources only; assertions and source goldens are unchanged.
- `stable-health.json`, `stable-health-final.json`, and `stable-discovery-manifest.json` retain the normal stable baseline. They do not establish candidate health.
- `candidate-startup.json` records normal installed Explorer startup, its catalog-collision refusal and the absence of a ready health response. The Java exit remains a failure; packaged SHA metadata is not relabeled as a health response.
- `native-control-summary.json` records all six objectives, exact report hashes, populations/exclusions and verdict changes. `common-native-names.json` contains the 3,466-name intersection. The diagnostic explicitly excludes extensions and correction plans on BOTH sides. It cannot replace the blocked default catalog, live service, or ordinary Judith comparison.
- `mechanical-comparison-summary.json` retains section counts and changed names, distinguishing raw differences from separately identified runtime-URL normalization. Native structures, source identity, outcomes and evidence bindings are not normalized. `compare-common.py` is the comparison algorithm.
- `overlap-comparison.json` compares twelve freshly serialized pairs. `overlap-source-inventory.json` is the earlier static source inventory at its explicitly recorded revisions. Matching names, Oracle-text strings, or script trees do not establish identity, source fidelity, or transferable evidence/qualification.
- `log-manifest.json` hashes original local command logs, including failed/cancelled attempts. Full raw logs, JSON reports, definition exports, traces/screenshots and archived XMLs remain in the original workspace's root/generated directories. Local absolute paths identify those observations; they are not portable download links.

`CompareNativePopulation.java` and `InspectIntegration.java` are one-off read-only diagnostic harnesses compiled against the installed application distribution on each side. They do not modify the application's default catalog or acceptance rules. `verify.just` and `canonical-catalog-resources.init.gradle` preserve the original verification commands. Their paths assume the original sibling-worktree layout and the workspace `.integration-tools` directory; restore that layout before replaying. Keep Gradle builds serialized, JDK 21 selected, candidate SHA/origin/clean-source guards enabled, and automatic daemon reaping disabled.

Core/era counts, SDK counts, catalog records and focused regression counts describe overlapping or different test populations. Do not sum focused regressions into broad totals, treat conditional branches as independent cards, or infer complete MTG rules fidelity from passing tests.
