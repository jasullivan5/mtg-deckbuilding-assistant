# Current research boundaries

[README](../README.md#current-state-and-authority) owns current status; [objective direction](OBJECTIVE_MODEL.md) separates adopted direction from unvalidated hypotheses.

- Discovery covers eligible authored native definitions and application extensions, not every Commander-legal Magic card. Unknown format legality/full identity is excluded explicitly before mechanical extraction.
- Current Discover requires a single fixed-identity commander. Partner/Background pairs, pre-game chosen identities, objective-first commander discovery, and whole-deck legality are not implemented.
- MATCH is conditional structural potential. Costs, legal choices, targets, resources, timing, replacement effects and other game state can prevent the actual outcome. No emitted pathway is not a proven nonmatch.
- Direct original creature-card graveyard-to-battlefield movement is bounded. Linked intermediate zones, complex attachment/state predicates and insufficient bindings can remain partial or UNKNOWN.
- Linked movement evidence supports bounded adjacent singular-target battlefield/exile/battlefield instructions and exact source-linked root exile/owner-return declarations, including one bounded resolution-time optional-exile wrapper. Structural relationships do not establish successful exile or return. Collections, nonadjacent or optional immediate continuations, general delayed continuations and ambiguous source-pile producers remain unresolved. The native timing correction distinguishes independent triggered return from source-duration return. Remaining `ExileUntilLeaves` definitions remain individually unreviewed beyond the bounded source cohort; matching a native pile does not certify Oracle timing or qualify a whole card. Root singular duration evidence is bounded; other anchors, additional abilities and collection durations retain UNKNOWN. [Linked movement history](LINKED_MOVEMENTS.md) · [Timing correction](EXILE_RETURN_TIMING.md) · [Legacy-helper audit](LEGACY_EXILE_SOURCE_FIDELITY.md)
- Union-target provenance supports a single fixed object requirement with explicit clause filters and source zones. Collection lineage supports bounded action-local producers and explicit consumers, including native moved-result bindings. Empty membership remains possible; requested selection cardinality is distinct from actual membership. Dynamic counts, correlated target groups, unresolved choosers, complements, separate resolutions, deferred consumers and general collection algebra remain UNKNOWN. Source-linked exile piles are separate engine-owned relationships. Structural lineage does not certify a native translation or successful arrival. [Collection provenance](COLLECTION_PROVENANCE.md)
- Specialized token operations such as Amass/populate and complex copying remain unsupported or unresolved. Positive quantity is conditional possibility, not the current game-state count. Token-granted abilities are not recursively attributed to the source.
- Single-zone triggered sources now respect their declared active zone; multi-zone trigger/source composition retains its prior bounded treatment. The bounded self-return input query now consumes these facts; that addition does not prove repeatable fuel or a complete sacrifice/return cycle. See [source-zone and sacrifice boundaries](OBJECTIVE_COVERAGE.md) and [adopted objective direction](OBJECTIVE_MODEL.md).
- Triggered event relationships cover draw, token creation, fixed forced sacrifice, direct fixed life changes and bounded direct player damage. Unknown player or object bindings, dynamic quantities, batch deaths, extra conditions and modal, deferred or reflexive links can remain UNKNOWN. Coupled life drain and general replacement effects are outside this interpretation. Shared records also cover operations from nondeath events, such as creature entry. [Triggered operations evidence](TRIGGERED_OPERATIONS_DISCOVERY.md)
- Creature input supply is bounded to intrinsic original-creature self-return, not generic reanimation or all creature production. Multi-face types, source-cost transitions, active-zone unions, extra conditions, opaque following steps and replacement limits can remain UNKNOWN. Finality is a barrier to a qualifying later death while present; a native return route is not unlimited recursion. No loop solver or assumed sacrifice pairing is implemented.
- Death-history discovery interprets supported root resolution branches, simple activation gates, intervening-if checks and bounded mana reductions at payment. It preserves native alternatives, prerequisites, scope and checkpoint. Compound or nested dependencies and unsupported payment or effect shapes remain unresolved. The legacy death-enhanced removal projection retains its magnitude rules and admissions. [Conditional causal evidence](CONDITIONAL_CAUSAL_DISCOVERY.md) · [Conditional payments](CONDITIONAL_COSTS.md)
- Fixed generic self-cast reductions can record a caster-relative battlefield existence condition with the full native filter. Support is bounded to selected permanent type, subtype, legendary, keyword and power patterns. Compound filters, additional gating, dynamic payments, and combined or alternative prices can remain unresolved. A supported discount does not establish a current witness or an affordable cast. [Conditional payments](CONDITIONAL_COSTS.md)
- Fixed chosen-target casting discounts preserve separate generic and colored declarations and supported target bindings. Modal, dynamic, source-relative, or unsupported object predicates and target requirements can remain UNKNOWN. A compatible target does not establish an actual selection, affordability, or successful resolution. The application derives isolated prices and leaves combined payment to Argentum.
- Aura attachment evidence covers bounded permanent enchant restrictions, attached-host constant stat modifications and attached step-trigger player operations. Other static or triggered dependencies, changed enchant abilities, nonbattlefield/player hosts and unsupported filters remain unresolved. Bounded granted activated abilities have separate evidence below. Casting choices, current attachments and trigger-time player bindings are different evidence. [Attachment evidence](AURA_ATTACHMENTS.md)
- Static granted activated abilities cover bounded front-definition creature Auras, Equipment hosts and plain creature / creatures-you-control groups. Native projection establishes current availability; legal activation, payment and resolution remain native checks. Nested bodies retain receiving-source and activating-player context without entering intrinsic Discover admission. Same-ID declarations on one granter, unexposed equal suppliers, broader filters, conditional/nested grants, loyalty, nonbattlefield, temporary and donor/copy-derived forms remain UNKNOWN or untraversed. Full layer/timestamp interaction and dynamic-source evaluation are outside interpretation. [Granted-ability evidence](GRANTED_ABILITIES.md)
- Static granted triggers retain separate granter, recipient, event, captured controller and stack evidence. Bounded creature attachments and plain creature groups are structurally interpreted; exact observations require native source generations, a unique event context and supplying-granter evidence. Same-ID declarations on one granter, intrinsic/copy/runtime collisions, unstamped group suppliers, changed granter visits and ambiguous event batches remain UNKNOWN. Soulbond, conditional/nested grants, temporary/copy-derived forms, general layers and full reflexive composition remain outside the subset. Nested operations do not enter intrinsic Discover admission. [Granted-trigger evidence](GRANTED_TRIGGERS.md)
- Native actions/facts and objective acceptance are distinct. Objective A retains its legacy death/removal projection alongside the new triggered response projection; shared action IDs do not prove whole-action causality. There is no general satisfiability solver or additional Oracle mechanics parser.
- Counter placement covers supported native instructions and entry declarations. Counter modification covers signed additive replacements and native doubling over supported recipient patterns, including supported Class-level declarations. Effects-only replacements require a known effect origin for concrete interpretation. State-dependent bindings, opaque quantities and limits, other factors, and competing replacement order remain outside supported interpretation. [Counter placement evidence](COUNTER_PLACEMENT_DISCOVERY.md)
- Class availability records native progression and the minimum active level. Level-transition trigger placeholders, general Class static effects, and Class token replacements remain unresolved or outside interpretation. The records do not plan progression or guarantee an unlock. [Class availability evidence](CLASS_AVAILABILITY.md)
- Pre-integration reports containing the retired SDK event LandTappedForMana fail current typed deserialization. The Aura verification reproduced the same error on unchanged main and the candidate. A merged-baseline report remains readable. These are separate revision-bound compatibility observations. [Attachment verification](AURA_ATTACHMENTS.md)
- Author/source-led tests and bounded native scenarios are not independent whole-card certification. Tatterkite remains blocked by its retained concern; other application records remain provisional without independent approval.
- Explorer retains only the latest run. New discovery expires old evidence URLs, including other tabs. Full exports can exceed 150 MB with the expanded native catalog; catalog loading takes time. Verified images require matching printing and face metadata. Missing, conflicting or unsupported identities and unavailable networks receive fallbacks; supported image layouts are deliberately bounded.
- The Explorer supports structural discovery and curated native Lab positions, not a deck editor, arbitrary scenario sandbox, strategic ranking or recommendation system. Windows launch and bounded browser checks are recorded; other platforms and exhaustive accessibility are not claimed tested.

- Legacy Explorer evidence records are often card-level and historical, with no mapping of individual scenario outcomes to the selected ability. The newer scope-aware verification index maps attributable requirements and observations, but does not establish whole-card completeness. Source review, successful bounded execution and independent approval remain different claims; complex conditions may need the exact retained records.
- Objective construction, production attribute selectors, Include/Require/Exclude composition, and guided exploration remain future capabilities. Existing objective-specific admission rules still apply. The adopted rules-text requirement and discovery-wide basic-land exclusion describe product policy. [Discovery model and policy](OBJECTIVE_MODEL.md#focused-admission-and-basic-lands)
- Probability/resource-access experiments, objective-aware pilots, controlled substitutions, representative native deck simulation and pressure/disruption testing remain future research. No explicit pairwise strategic synergy model is currently required; whether the empirical approach supplies enough strategic guidance is unvalidated. Outcomes would depend on mechanics, policies, baselines and environment.

The [separate correction](TAP_PAYMENT_CORRECTION.md) merged in engine PR #7 and application PR #44; old receipts remain revision-bound, and bounded corrected observations do not certify every activation cost. This research increment does not alter payment code or evidence.
