# Current research boundaries

[README](../README.md#current-state-and-authority) owns current status; [objective direction](OBJECTIVE_MODEL.md) separates adopted direction from unvalidated hypotheses.

- Discovery covers eligible authored native definitions and application extensions, not every Commander-legal Magic card. Unknown format legality/full identity is excluded explicitly before mechanical extraction.
- Supported selection is a single fixed-identity commander. Partner/Background pairs, pre-game chosen identities and whole-deck legality are not implemented.
- MATCH is conditional structural potential. Costs, legal choices, targets, resources, timing, replacement effects and other game state can prevent the actual outcome. No emitted pathway is not a proven nonmatch.
- Direct original creature-card graveyard-to-battlefield movement is bounded. Linked intermediate zones, complex attachment/state predicates and insufficient bindings can remain partial or UNKNOWN.
- Specialized token operations such as Amass/populate and complex copying remain unsupported or unresolved. Positive quantity is conditional possibility, not the current game-state count. Token-granted abilities are not recursively attributed to the source.
- Single-zone triggered sources now respect their declared active zone; multi-zone trigger/source composition retains its prior bounded treatment. The bounded self-return input query now consumes these facts; that addition does not prove repeatable fuel or a complete sacrifice/return cycle. See [source-zone and sacrifice boundaries](OBJECTIVE_COVERAGE.md) and [adopted objective direction](OBJECTIVE_MODEL.md).
- Triggered event relationships cover draw, token creation, fixed forced sacrifice, direct fixed life changes and bounded direct player damage. Unknown player or object bindings, dynamic quantities, batch deaths, extra conditions and modal, deferred or reflexive links can remain UNKNOWN. Coupled life drain and general replacement effects are outside this interpretation. Shared records also cover operations from nondeath events, such as creature entry. [Triggered operations evidence](TRIGGERED_OPERATIONS_DISCOVERY.md)
- Creature input supply is bounded to intrinsic original-creature self-return, not generic reanimation or all creature production. Multi-face types, source-cost transitions, active-zone unions, extra conditions, opaque following steps and replacement limits can remain UNKNOWN. Finality is a barrier to a qualifying later death while present; a native return route is not unlimited recursion. No loop solver or assumed sacrifice pairing is implemented.
- Death-history discovery interprets supported root resolution branches, simple activation gates and intervening-if checks. It preserves native alternatives, branch polarity, prerequisites, scope, checkpoint and links to the existing operation evidence. Distinct supported operations can qualify even when their effects differ in kind or direction. Compound or nested dependencies, unsupported payloads, conditional discounts and other check locations remain unresolved or outside interpretation. Count checks are diagnostic. Source-ability resolution history remains internal. The legacy death-enhanced removal projection retains its magnitude rules and admissions. See [conditional causal evidence](CONDITIONAL_CAUSAL_DISCOVERY.md).
- Native actions/facts and objective acceptance are distinct. Objective A retains its legacy death/removal projection alongside the new triggered response projection; shared action IDs do not prove whole-action causality. There is no general satisfiability solver or additional Oracle mechanics parser.
- Author/source-led tests and bounded native scenarios are not independent whole-card certification. Tatterkite remains blocked by its retained concern; other application records remain provisional without independent approval.
- Explorer retains only the latest run. New discovery expires old evidence URLs, including other tabs. Full exports can be about 40–49 MB; catalog loading takes time. Verified images require matching printing and face metadata. Missing, conflicting or unsupported identities and unavailable networks receive fallbacks; supported image layouts are deliberately bounded.
- The Explorer supports structural discovery and curated native Lab positions, not a deck editor, arbitrary scenario sandbox, strategic ranking or recommendation system. Windows launch and bounded browser checks are recorded; other platforms and exhaustive accessibility are not claimed tested.

- Legacy Explorer evidence records are often card-level and historical, with no mapping of individual scenario outcomes to the selected ability. The newer scope-aware verification index maps attributable requirements and observations, but does not establish whole-card completeness. Source review, successful bounded execution and independent approval remain different claims; complex conditions may need the exact retained records.
- General relationship classification/filtering, constructable objectives, selective layering and complementary-objective suggestions remain unimplemented. The building-block framing and optional role descriptions have bounded source-led research support, not catalog-wide validation or measured user-comprehension gains. Grouping does not prove an interaction; an interaction does not establish contextual value.
- Probability/resource-access experiments, objective-aware pilots, controlled substitutions, representative native deck simulation and pressure/disruption testing remain future research. No explicit pairwise strategic synergy model is currently required; whether the empirical approach supplies enough strategic guidance is unvalidated. Outcomes would depend on mechanics, policies, baselines and environment.
- The [phased examination](COST_EXAMINATION.md) retains two original payment contradictions at engine `84fb6147`: tapped Mind Stone mana and Evolving Wilds completed under Explicit without paying the tap. The [separate correction](TAP_PAYMENT_CORRECTION.md) merged in engine PR #7 and application PR #44; old receipts remain revision-bound, and bounded corrected observations do not certify every activation cost. This research increment does not alter payment code or evidence.

Completed phase reports, old counts and reproduction instructions belong to the movable historical export. [Repository layout](REPOSITORY_LAYOUT.md) distinguishes that material from retained current regression inputs and source evidence.
