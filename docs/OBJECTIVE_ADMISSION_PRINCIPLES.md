# General objective admission: Product Studio assessment

2026-11-10. **Research and recommendation, not an adopted admission rule. Production discovery, extraction, classifications and engine behavior are unchanged.**

Recommend a **revised, narrow input-supply principle**, while retaining objective-specific admission policy. Do not adopt either a universal ordinary-participation exclusion or a strict interaction-only veto. Require evidence of the particular relationship being claimed; allow one operation to serve interaction and objective production. Mechanics alone do not settle every primary-discovery judgment.

## A. Principles the evidence supports

1. **Eligibility is not a supplier relationship.** Having a required type/characteristic establishes that an object could be used, not that this card distinctively improves that resource's availability. Require a source/ability-bound operation or permission showing production, access, replenishment or changed usability, with the objective's exact resource constraints. Skeleton's original-card return is such a witness; Walking Corpse's creature type alone is not. This is a reusable evidence requirement across resource types, not a completed general supplier classifier.
2. **Do not apply that exclusion to other relationships automatically.** Printed power can establish a power-threshold state. An ordinary artifact contributes to an artifact-count state. An ordinary land play or creature casting can be exactly the event a player wants. Which such contributions should appear prominently is an objective-scope/product choice, not mechanical impossibility.
3. **Interactivity is not a mechanical veto.** An operation can interact with opponents and produce our anchor through the same effect. A sacrifice cost can provide a separate qualifying contribution within a removal ability. Damage to an opponent can be a payoff. Require the actual event/subject/recipient/branch relationship; do not require a different printed ability or assume that destroy/damage/untap has a fixed strategic purpose.

The stronger phrase “distinctive beyond ordinary participation” is not yet a strategy-neutral executable rule. Forest exposes the problem: its normal mana ability actually makes mana available. Rejecting it as *ordinary* mana supply requires a declared comparison baseline, not merely detecting an ability or change. The frozen expectation excluded ordinary Forest supply; this investigation qualifies that expectation rather than concealing the ambiguity. The narrower “type eligibility alone is insufficient evidence” survives; a universal exclusion of normal resource operations does not follow.

These recommendations refine the existing distinctive-contribution direction; they do not authorize changing Tragic Slip, admitting mass removal, or implementing contextual interaction selection.

## D. Mechanical feasibility and smallest gaps

Already available: physical/native identity and effective eligibility; original movement and self-binding; owner/controller/zones; source action, cost/effect and branch identity; sacrifice/damage/removal scopes; token metadata; draw/token event links; explicit mechanical versus policy decisions. These can require a witness instead of enumerating “ordinary creature/land/artifact” exclusions. In today's supplier query, ordinary bodies already emit no return link, so this recommendation eliminates no additional active policy branch by itself.

Missing information has several distinct meanings:

- **Primary-purpose meaning:** Murder can mechanically produce our death. Structural data does not prove why that is insufficient for primary discovery while a sacrifice cost is sufficient. The existing policy makes that product choice explicitly. The proposed interaction-only wording cannot replace it by inspection of operation names.
- **Affected population:** for a bounded reconsideration of mass producers, retain the native selector's targeting mode, all/each quantifier, explicit inclusion of our objects/player, required versus optional branches and applicable limits. An existential “can affect our creature” predicate loses the distinction between Wrath and Murder. Current native data contains these distinctions, but the legacy objective projection does not expose a general queryable contract for that decision.
- **Other supplier families:** Exploration needs interpreted permission/count/player/active-source semantics. Library-land movement exists, but typed land constraints and entry production are not a current objective query. Detecting any extra ability would not establish a useful resource witness.
- **Other anchors/outcomes:** state establishment needs printed/effective characteristics, recipient control, count and checkpoint; power/counter modification needs positive amount, recipient, duration and replacement-versus-initiation semantics. Death-conditioned untap/mana/life need their own supported outputs. The existing triggered draw/token interface must not be stretched to assert that replacements are triggers.

None requires an engine redesign in the inspected cohort. This study establishes no native executor defect and performs no engine work. It also does not settle Agent of Shauku's IsLand uncertainty or the earlier primary-inclusion boundary for narrow artifact/land sacrifice capabilities.

## E–F. Risks and recommendation

Recommend **option 3, revised/conditional forms, with option 4 retained where needed**: use the narrow supplier evidence principle; reject universal ordinary-participation exclusion; treat overlapping interaction/production as legitimate; retain current specialized death policies until a bounded behavioral change is separately chosen. Do not adopt both proposals as universal admission rules.

The main risks are hiding meaningful payoffs through a whole-card/whole-branch interaction label; admitting ordinary removal by equating possibility with primary relevance; calling every mana/entry operation distinctive without a baseline; excluding legitimate printed state establishers; conflating missing definitions or unresolved mechanics with policy rejection; and claiming complete loops or deck value from structural links. A player can understand “this ability returns its original creature” or “this effect includes your creatures”; “interaction-only” without the objective and intended use is less reliable. No comprehension study was run.

## G. Next implementation decision — proposed, not begun

Authorize, if desired, a bounded **own-inclusive mass death-production relationship**. Reuse the walker and native affected-set/each-player data, expose a queryable selector/quantifier/required-branch link, and revise only the death objective's specialized opposing-removal exclusion for supported untargeted all-creature destruction and each-player creature sacrifice that explicitly include us.

Expected behavioral changes to demonstrate: **Damnation and Fleshbag Marauder** become direct producers in Judith; **Wrath of God** becomes a direct producer in a white-eligible context such as Kenrith. These are proposed primary-admission changes, not findings that current NO_MATCH is mechanical impossibility. Keep Murder excluded by the existing friendly-target policy; preserve Attrition/Bombardment cost matches, all suppliers/draw-token beneficiaries, Tragic Slip's separate historical branch, and all other objectives. Do not automatically move enhanced removal into a nonexistent contextual selector.

Controls should include opponent-only sweeps, noncreature-only destruction, optional choose-opponent branches, token-only affected sets, mandatory own-inclusive sacrifice, unresolved type/branch intersections, same-operation overlap, indestructibility and exile replacements. Use source-first contracts, shared extraction tests, bounded native scenarios and complete unchanged-pool comparisons across all four objectives, followed by real Discover inspection. Do not infer guaranteed deaths from destruction or require another printed effect. This is one substantial, bounded application increment that tests a conditional principle without a general interaction classifier. **No such implementation has begun.**
