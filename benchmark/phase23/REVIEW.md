# Phase 2.3 source review

Frozen before extraction edits. SHA-256: `988a48b7e8d08874b6495a22753325cb2a52876ede2e42c4b7a5259bf15efe6c`. No expectations were subsequently changed. This is source-led review by the implementing reviewer, independent of search output; no separate human adjudication is claimed. Current Scryfall Oracle and relevant rulings are archived verbatim as source payloads. sources.json identifies the sources, while review-v1.json preserves native paths, card identities, expected roles and reasoning. The existing Phase 2.2 frozen benchmark is unchanged and remains a separate regression set.

13 native pathway cases / 13 actual authored cards: 11 expected accepted contributions, one negative absence (Diregraf Scavenger), one explicit UNKNOWN interpreter boundary (Incandescent Soulstoke). **Soulstoke is known from Oracle to be capable of the desired death.** Its expected UNKNOWN preserves the still-uninterpreted Elemental restriction, rather than calling the rules uncertain or silently dropping the filter. Thus the final source-positive examples accepted are 11/12, with the twelfth conservatively unresolved; the software-contract denominator of 11 must not be advertised as complete real-mechanic recall. The two 'excluded' contract cases are not both mechanical negatives.

Held-out examples reserved for transfer validation: Final Flourish, Souls of the Lost, Morkrut Behemoth, Through the Breach and Incandescent Soulstoke. Their expectations were established from sources before the corresponding code changes, and the implementation was not designed around their card names. Cabal Therapy was fetched as a possible example but is absent from the pinned authored source/catalog; it is not an integration fixture. All source responses, including unused/gap examples, remain identified. Lava Dart is an additional unresolved filter/support-grade boundary, not a verified accepted creature-death card.

Consequential source distinctions: Dread Return's target is chosen before its flashback sacrifices; three creatures produce three death events if not replaced, while discovery returns one cost pathway with count=3. Lash's native ModalEffect is a cost fork with countsAsModalSpell=false, not an Oracle modal spell. Dusk Mangler's casting payment and ETB are distinct actions. Optional selection, successful entry, same-object survival and required control constrain delayed sacrifice. Cauldron Dance's graveyard creature returns to hand; its separately put hand creature is sacrificed. Diregraf Scavenger's exile and player life changes are not creature death/removal.
