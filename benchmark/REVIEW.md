# Frozen source-led benchmark

Reviewed before corresponding production behavior changes, using Scryfall Oracle/rulings and native pinned authored SDK definitions. Expectations were not derived from search output. The same implementing reviewer conducted the review; no independent human adjudication is claimed. No expectations were subsequently corrected. Native source links, Oracle identities, prerequisites and required fields are in review-v1.json; complete authoritative responses are archived in oracle/. sources.json maps every reviewed card to its archived source.

Freeze SHA-256: `3f28796f6beda350e38f85c56886d47c44ddf6cea0edb5c4eae1007e67932f30`. 29 pathway cases / 23 actual cards. All candidate named cards exist in the combined catalog; Judith is explicitly application-owned, not upstream-authored. Pit Raptor, Lord of the Pit, Muxus, Bloodline Culling, Ambush Gigapede and Erg Raiders add payment, dynamic sign, restricted and unresolved cases. Synthetic tests are separate Kotlin fixtures and do not establish authored coverage.

Mechanical MATCH means potential under legal conditions, not guaranteed gameplay. Mandatory end-step triggers are not described as SDK delayed-trigger objects. Accursed Centaur may sacrifice itself; Demonic Taskmaster excludes itself and its triggered ability persists independently of its source. Victimize needs the selected target/collection state and a sacrifice during resolution. Reduction needs sufficient resources/toughness; damage needs lethal magnitude and no prevention. Fleshbag mechanically generates own deaths, but our current opposing-removal relationship policy excludes it. Blood Artist/Gravecrawler are not direct generators. Judith is a conditional death-triggered removal consumer; her ordinary damage path is not a direct generator. Erg Raiders remains unresolved because the authored entered-this-turn condition does not fully encode gained-control history.

Native Assay compile was also actually attempted on all 23 archived Oracle payloads: 15 READ_WHOLE, 8 DECLINED. Raw output and exit codes are in verification/phase2_2/assay-summary.json and assay/. These are corroboration only, not script-equivalence proofs or production loaders.
