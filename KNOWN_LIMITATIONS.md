# Phase 2.5 boundaries

- Objective C supports native creature templates, registered predefined templates, batch groups, source copies and target copies. Amass, populate, chosen/equipped copy primitives and other specialized token operations remain unsupported. Token-granted abilities are retained in native evidence but are not recursively attributed as executable actions of the source card.
- Predefined characteristics come from pinned PredefinedTokens definitions. Unknown registry names stay UNKNOWN. Copy type replacement uses merged native CopyExceptions; clear source/target types are supported, while complex/contradictory filters, state predicates and unbound/moved source references remain UNKNOWN. Current creature status alone does not guarantee copiable creature characteristics.
- Positive quantity means a conditionally possible positive native count/X expression, not the current count. Zero/negative fixed or provably zero expressions fail. Stored quantities and unresolved conditional magnitude expressions remain UNKNOWN. Resources/choices and a positive evaluated quantity are explicit prerequisites; no complete satisfiability solver is claimed.
- Each token variant/group has its own type, controller, quantity, conditions and action identity. Opponent/noncreature alternatives cannot be mixed into a match. Batch support is validated by native fixtures; no authored batch example was located at the pin.
- The frozen token benchmark has 16 reviewed pathways across 15 real cards, with three held-out cases and one unresolved Amass case. It is source-led by the implementing reviewer, not externally adjudicated. Its results are not whole-catalog recall. Krenko's Command, Bitterblossom and Tempt with Vengeance remain absent from the pinned upstream catalog. Phase 2.7 supplies the first two through verified application-owned native extensions; Tempt with Vengeance remains missing. See docs/CARD_COVERAGE.md for the independent external-pool coverage and its uncertainty boundaries.
- Forty new tests complement 170 retained tests. Three added engine scenarios verify only their recorded interactions. Extraction/serialization does not establish engine execution for every returned result.
- Full historical/generated snapshots are excluded from the lean ZIP. Compact manifests and source-restoration/regeneration scripts replace that bulk. Python3/patch are needed only for optional evidence regeneration; normal build/run uses Git/JDK21/Gradle wrapper.

## Preserved earlier boundaries

# Phase 2.4 boundaries

- Direct original-card graveyard→battlefield movement is supported; linked intermediate-zone causality (Living Death) is retained as facts, not composed into the initial objective. Do not interpret its nonacceptance as absence of source-level reanimation capability.
- General Gate.WhenCondition satisfiability is not implemented. Conditions and both branches are retained; six initially accepted paths were downgraded to UNKNOWN. Other conditional native success/payment pathways can qualify with explicit prerequisites.
- Attached-object predicates (Animate Dead), mana-value/subtype/state/property filters (including Unearth), relative players, some collection membership and dynamic destination variants remain partial. There is no universal object/condition satisfiability solver.
- Ownership, source-zone player and destination controller remain distinct. Native owner-default entry is correlated with the chosen owner. Trigger-controller rebinding and insufficient bindings retain UNKNOWN; arbitrary game-state control changes are not simulated.
- Successful earlier target/collection movement is an explicit assumption. Replacements, failed movements, leaving/reentering, hidden choices and legal-target loss can invalidate the represented path. Moving the original physical card still creates a new MTG game object; no continuous-object identity claim is made.
- A's legacy death/removal/dependency interpretation remains in the existing extractor. B adds no second native walker, but this pass does not fully generalize every A-specific concept.
- Support labels are local structural variants/objective conclusions, not whole-card or whole-SDK-class verification. Cross-objective class summaries contain mixed variants; use fingerprint coverage reports for detail. Neither-query-accepts is not synonymous with unsupported.
- Missing-fact and missing-objective diagnostic warnings are suspected gaps. Generic unhandled mechanics, known policy exclusions and eligibility-data gaps are separate. No unreviewed warning is a confirmed false negative.
- Frozen review expectations are source-led by the implementing reviewer, not external adjudication. Two originally held-out labels were design-exposed and are reported as source-informed transfer. UNKNOWN boundaries are not true negatives. Three named candidate cards are absent from the authored catalog.
- Only the recorded native engine scenarios are gameplay-verified. No general deck simulation, optimization, strategic desirability score or broader package/recursion discovery was added.
- Linux/JDK21 was executed; Windows/macOS setup instructions were not executed. The original application archive had no established application Git history; source manifests identify deliveries. Upstream remains pinned and clean.

## Earlier Phase 2.3 limitations (retained)

# Known limitations — Phase 2.3

- MATCH is conditional structural potential, not a guaranteed death, available target, payable action, resolved whole-board state or strategic recommendation. Resources, applicable zones, successful movement, required control, same-object survival, lethal magnitude and absence of replacement/prevention remain prerequisites.
- Four bounded interpretation families were added; there is no exhaustive SDK interpreter. SUPPORTED grades a recognized local variant/conclusion. A card or enclosing action can remain partial; recognized costs link to associated spell resolution for completeness checks. Unknown filters and children cannot be erased by a supported parent.
- Flashback/optional/self-alternative cost routes and root spell mode/Choice/OrPay costs are traversed. Other keyword lowering, nested/non-casting mode costs, extra-mode costs, Forage and other unhandled native costs remain visible. Availability conditions and complete payment legality are not solved globally. Multiple sacrifices are one action/pathway with native count, not multiple invented pathways.
- Raw collection-size conditions retain thresholds and branch requirements. Filtered/distinct-type guards, unknown cardinalities/bindings, relative/complement selection, aliases across branches and many source variants remain partial. Collection-produced death-event causality is not automatically translated into death-enhanced removal.
- Collection zone flow models a conditional successful-arrival branch, with failed/replaced movement explicitly possible. Original collections retain IDs rather than proving successful-move counts; filtered partitions can span zones and stay UNKNOWN. Indexed targets need an existing selected object and same-object survival. Face-down characteristic changes, owner-to-controller relations, dynamic destinations, battlefield-to-battlefield semantics and grant/replacement lowering remain unsupported/partial.
- Positive artifact compatibility permits artifact creatures while preserving noncreature/token/controller restrictions. Other property/subtype satisfiability is incomplete. Incandescent Soulstoke is known from Oracle to be a real death capability but remains UNKNOWN because the Elemental filter is retained. Lava Dart's land/subtype filter is similarly a partial boundary; no runtime card list substitutes for solving it.
- Ordinary friendly-target removal and incidental symmetrical opposing removal remain policy-excluded separately from mechanics. Some symmetrical payment/sacrifice structures still require relationship review (including Killing Wave); a preserved local capability does not establish the quality of its policy qualification. No utility/payoff requirement or desirability score is applied.
- Damage/toughness deaths require actual state thresholds. Unsupported dynamic signs stay UNKNOWN. No gameplay simulation, deck evaluation, recursion search, package discovery or optimization is implemented. Reanimation/entry is inspected only as a prerequisite to a later death where present.
- Source-led review is independent of search output but conducted by the implementing reviewer, not separately human-adjudicated. The old 29-case contract has one unresolved Erg Raiders history-fidelity case. The new 13-case contract includes 11 expected accepted pathways, one negative and one explicit UNKNOWN boundary; source-positive acceptance is 11/12, not 100% recall. Unreviewed additions and warnings are not confirmed errors. Cabal Therapy is absent from the authored catalog.
- Independent coverage traverses typed serialized authored nodes, not every non-polymorphic container, possible SDK subtype, runtime grant or engine-lowered action. New support grades and fuller explanation fields change the inventory; group-count increases need not be coverage regressions. Root fingerprints are diagnostic normalizations, not proofs of semantic equivalence.
- Regression identities can be ambiguous after structural refactoring or duplicate native paths. Explanations, constraints, verdicts and support-schema additions are distinguished, but prerequisite prose edits are not semantically proved equivalent. Attribution exposes observed layers, not isolated causal experiments or quality scores.
- Commander metadata gaps remain conservatively excluded and reported separately from mechanics. Single fixed-identity commanders are supported; pairs/backgrounds/chosen-color and other construction exceptions are not approximated. Full identity includes hybrid and faces; conventional color search is not Commander eligibility. Judith and Oviya Pashiri, Sage Lifecrafter are application-owned extensions with explicit provenance and bounded engine scenarios; see docs/COMMANDER_AUTHORING.md.
- Linux/JDK21 was executed; Windows/macOS setup is documented but not executed. Clean setup needs GitHub, Gradle and Maven access. Hosted verification used a temporary JDK and trusted public proxy CA, with no TLS bypass, downloaded environment, cache or credentials packaged. Historical phase reports describe earlier behavior; use current Phase 2.5 summaries for current findings.

- Phase 2.9 applies seven individually source-verified eligibility corrections through an effective metadata resolver; raw native definitions remain unchanged. Hundreds of other metadata discrepancies and 37 identity-review candidates remain uncorrected. Corrected metadata does not certify native mechanics; single fixed-identity construction limits still apply. See docs/ELIGIBILITY_CORRECTIONS.md. Windows/JDK21 full builds and controlled CLI comparisons are now executed; older environment notes above are historical.
